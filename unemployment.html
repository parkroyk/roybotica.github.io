Economic Cascade Model

2026 Forecast Boundary

Simulation parameters only affect the years 2026 through 2034. Historical and near-term baseline data (2014–2025) remains fixed, creating a realistic divergence point in the future.

Dynamic CPI (Phillips Curve)

Inflation is now modeled dynamically. If unemployment rises significantly, consumer demand drops, causing inflation to cool (lower CPI). This is calculated automatically, but can be manually scaled further using the Inflation Override slider.

Consumer Confidence Shock

Rising unemployment causes a rapid, non-linear drop in consumer confidence. Even employed citizens tighten spending preemptively. The sensitivity slider controls how sharply confidence collapses per percentage point of unemployment increase.

Birth Rate & Demographic Drag

Economic uncertainty suppresses birth rates. While babies don't become workers overnight, a declining birth rate immediately impacts child-related industries and signals long-term structural drag. The model applies a compounding reduction to GDP and consumer base size that worsens over the 10-year forecast horizon.
